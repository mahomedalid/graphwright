The run objective is TOPOLOGY VALIDATION, not fitting data to an unquestioned specification.
Treat the current topology as hypotheses. Test keys, nullability, properties, relationship
endpoints, cardinalities and constraints against source evidence, including already approved
or pending mappings. Existing mapping coverage is NOT validation coverage.
Use record_topology_finding to persist supported, mismatch, or inconclusive findings.
Cite evidence ids and state exactly which assumption was tested and the recommended edit.
For missing entities or relationships use node:ProposedName or edge:PROPOSED_NAME and aspect
'addition'; recommend additions only with source evidence and confirmed business meaning.
Distinguish source quality, sample limitations, and naming ambiguity from topology mistakes.
Absence of overlap, especially in independent samples, never proves a relationship invalid.
Do not narrow many-side cardinality merely because a sample contains unique values.
Never edit topology or approve facts. Recommendations are advisory and require human review.
Do not declare DONE until each in-scope node or edge has a persisted finding, or explicitly
record why it could not be tested. Include untested constraints and remaining gaps in DETAILS.
If discovery scope is EDGES ONLY, all validation and addition recommendations must be edge-only.
Existing node mappings are endpoint context, not node-discovery or node-validation targets.

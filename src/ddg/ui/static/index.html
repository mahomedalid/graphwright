Previous builders

    Review an older approval

    Confirm that this mapping still has the intended business meaning in the current graph definition. The app will check source columns, schema, and endpoint ID structure again before saving.

    This does not rerun data profiling, uniqueness checks, or join evidence, and does not repair mappings. If the mapping is wrong or uncertain, cancel and leave it out of the build instead.

    Delete an approved fact

    Use this for a fact that cannot be corrected or revalidated. It will be removed from active knowledge and future builders, and its candidate approval will be revoked. Audit history is retained. Dependent facts are not deleted.

    Previously generated builders and graph snapshots are unchanged. Generate and review a new builder after deletion.
